Design rule

No safety decision depends on the uplink, the cloud or an AI model. The one single point of failure we accept is the zone edge node: it is detected by its neighbour and covered by an operating restriction, and a zone moves to an independent alarm controller or paired nodes if the ADR-009 revisit triggers fire. Evidence status: Designed. Hardware drills are part of the ADR-009 assurance plan; nothing here is hardware-tested yet.
